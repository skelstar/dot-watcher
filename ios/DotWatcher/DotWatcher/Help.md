# What do DotWatcher do?

DotWatcher shares your location in real time so other runners in the session know where you are, and spectators can follow you on a map during a race or event.

## Setup

**Sign in** — use your DotWatcher account so the server can check which sessions you can read or write.

**Choose a session** — if someone gave you an invite code, enter it to join as a viewer. Only create a new session if you're organizing one yourself; that makes you the owner. Tracking requires an owner or runner membership; owners can promote invited viewers to runners.

## Tracking

Tap **Start Tracking** to begin sharing your location. Your position is sent every 15 seconds.

Tap **Stop** when you're done. You can resume the tracking.

## Viewing the map

Once you've selected a session, a link appears below it. Tap **Open map in browser** to view the live map.

Share the same link with spectators so they can watch from any device.

## Privacy and Terms

Privacy Policy: https://dot-watcher.skelstar.io/privacy

Terms of Use: https://dot-watcher.skelstar.io/terms

Use **Account > Delete account** to delete your DotWatcher account, memberships, owned sessions, and stored location rows linked to your account. Historical records without account attribution, admin uploads, or operational logs may need manual deletion.

Do not use DotWatcher for emergency response, rescue, medical, safety-critical tracking, or any situation where delayed or missing location data could cause harm.

## Battery

Keep an eye on the battery indicator — continuous GPS use drains the battery faster than normal. 15 seconds is a good trade-off. For longer events, this interval will be extended.
