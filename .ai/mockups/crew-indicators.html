Crew indicators

Two status lines under the count in the main-screen "Your crew" row: codes you've sent that nobody has used yet, and people who joined since you last opened the crew.

State

"Waiting" = a crew code you made that is unused and unexpired. "Joined" = someone redeemed one of your codes; it clears when you open the crew. People you add yourself by entering their code never count as new.

New faces get a blue ring on the row. The ring is an extra cue only; the text line carries the meaning.
