Crew on the main screen

A crew is the people you have chosen: you add each other by hand, and from then on you always see each other by name. This mock-up tries to make that clear without a manual. Tap through the phone, and use the buttons to jump to a state.

What it is trying to say

  • Crew is a list you keep, so it gets a row on the main screen, not only a line inside a card.
  • The ring means crew. A circle with a dark ring is someone in your crew, everywhere: the row, the card pictures, later the map.
  • Adding is a handshake. You send a request, they accept, then you see each other by name. Until then they show as “Pending”.
  • “Crew” and “invite” mean different things. Crew is every time; an invite is one session.

Still to decide

  • Mutual or one-way? Friends implies both agree. Following someone is simpler, but it is not the same as friendship.
  • How do people find each other? An exact username, a short code, or a QR code when you are together. This mock-up uses an exact username.
  • Is a crew per account, or per session? This assumes per account, always there.

Names and people are examples. Nothing here talks to the server.
