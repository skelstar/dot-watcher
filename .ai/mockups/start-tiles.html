Start screen as tiles

Today’s start screen next to two tile layouts. Each keeps the park-sign colours, type and line icons and keeps Go biggest and lowest, where a thumb reaches. The small tiles take over the header icons and the crew row. Use the switches to see the tiles change.

What moves where

  • Header icons: Account (today’s person icon, which opens Settings) and Help become small tiles.
  • The crew row and “2 of your crew are out” line become the Crew tile, which shows who is out.
  • What’s new is a small tile with a count that appears only after an update. Tapping it here hides it, as opening the list would.
  • Events nearby become one wide orange tile above Go, so they stay prominent but never push Go off the bottom.
  • The wordmark moves to the top of the empty space; the grid sits at the bottom.
