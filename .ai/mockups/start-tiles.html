Start screen as tiles

Today’s start screen next to two tile layouts. The dot-watchr wordmark is the hero, and every tile, Go included, sits right under it on a 6-wide grid of squares. 1x1 tiles are icon only; bigger tiles add a title and a short line. Both tile layouts can be edited: press and hold a tile, tap its round handle to change size, and drag it to a new slot.

How it works

  • Grid: 6 squares across with an 8px gap. A 1x1 tile is about 41px on a 340px-wide phone; a 2x2 is about 90px.
  • Edit mode: press and hold a tile (or tap Edit tiles). The tile you held is selected and gets a round orange handle; tap the handle to cycle through its sizes. Drag any tile and dashed slots appear behind the grid, with the landing spot highlighted. Tap another tile to select it, and tap Done (or empty space) to finish. Layouts are remembered in this browser; Reset puts the starting layout back.
  • Allowed sizes: What’s new, Help and Account 1x1 or 2x2. Crew, Join and Create 1x1, 2x2 or 4x2. Go 2x2, 4x2, 6x2 or 6x3. Event 4x2 or 6x2.
  • Dropping a tile on others moves them to the first free space that fits, so two tiles of the same size swap. Everything then slides up, so the grid stays packed under the wordmark.
  • 1x1 tiles have no visible text, so they need accessibility labels. Counts show as plain number badges. On light tiles the badge is the dark accent (navy, sand number) and sits over the top-right corner; on Go it is the light accent (sand, navy number) inside the tile.
  • Go shows how many of your crew are out as the same number badge, so you can see it just before you start. It disappears at 0.
  • What’s new appears only after an update; tapping it here hides it. The event tile appears only when something is nearby.
  • First cut is light only: the Appearance setting (System / Light / Dark) comes out of Settings, and the app always draws light.
