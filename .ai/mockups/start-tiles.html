Start screen as tiles

The Tiles B layout with the dot-watchr wordmark in three places: on top (the earlier mock-up), in the middle, and at the bottom. With the wordmark lower down, the tiles sit right at the top of the screen. Every tile, Go included, is on a 6-wide grid of squares; 1x1 tiles are icon only, and bigger tiles add a title and a short line. All three can be edited: press and hold a tile, tap its round handle to change size, and drag it to a new slot.

How it works

  • Grid: 6 squares across with an 8px gap. A 1x1 tile is about 41px on a 340px-wide phone; a 2x2 is about 90px.
  • Edit mode: press and hold a tile (or tap Edit tiles). With a mouse you can also just drag a tile. The tile you held is selected and gets a round orange handle; tap the handle to cycle through its sizes. Drag any tile and dashed slots appear behind the grid, with the landing spot highlighted. Tap another tile to select it, and tap Done (or empty space) to finish. Layouts are remembered in this browser; Reset puts the starting layout back.
  • Account and Settings are separate. Account is about you and needs you signed in: who you’re signed in as, your crew name, blocked users, sign out, delete account (signed out, it’s the sign-in form). Settings is about this phone and works signed out: map dot size, satellite alerts, developer options, legal.
  • Allowed sizes: What’s new, Help, Account and Settings 1x1 or 2x2. Crew, Join and Create 1x1, 2x2 or 4x2. Go 2x2, 4x2, 6x2 or 6x3. Event 4x2 or 6x2.
  • Dropping a tile on others moves them to the first free space that fits, so two tiles of the same size swap. Everything then slides up, so the grid stays packed at the top.
  • Wordmark in the middle: it sits centred in the space left under the tiles, so it moves as the tiles grow or shrink. Wordmark at the bottom: it stays just above the home indicator, whatever the tiles do.
  • 1x1 tiles have no visible text, so they need accessibility labels. Counts show as plain number badges. On light tiles the badge is the dark accent (navy, sand number) and sits over the top-right corner; on Go it is the light accent (sand, navy number) inside the tile.
  • Go shows how many of your crew are out as the same number badge, so you can see it just before you start. It disappears at 0.
  • What’s new is always on the screen. After an update it shows a count of the changes since the version you last ran; opening it clears the count until the next update. The event tile appears only when something is nearby.
  • First cut is light only: the Appearance setting (System / Light / Dark) comes out of Settings, and the app always draws light.
