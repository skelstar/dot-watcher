Landing page: sign buttons

Replaces the green Go button with three full-width, rectangular "sign" buttons: Go, Invite code and Create. The screen background is white. Only Go is solid green with yellow text. The other two are cream with green text and a green frame. Each has a right-pointing arrow and a line of secondary text. Option C (hero Go) is the chosen layout. The secondary copy is placeholder.

Palette (sampled from the sign)

Sign green #243a30 Sign yellow #efbf3f Cream #f6efd9

Typeface: ITC Garamond Standard Bold (the real one is commercial)

Type sizes

Title 30sp bold. Secondary 15sp bold, about half the title size, the same ratio as the sign. Hero Go in option C is 56sp.

Option C: Hero Go

Go is the primary action, about 1.5x the height of the other two, and the three buttons sit at the bottom within thumb reach. Best if Go is used far more than the other two.

Notes for implementation

  • The sign uses ITC Garamond Standard Bold, which is a commercial font. EB Garamond is the closest free Garamond. Alegreya has a larger x-height, nearer to ITC Garamond's wide, sturdy look. To match the sign exactly, license ITC Garamond and bundle it in the iOS and Android apps.
  • Cream-on-green contrast: green #243a30 on cream #f6efd9 is about 10:1. Yellow #efbf3f on green is about 7:1. Both pass AA at all sizes.
  • Editing your name: tap the initials circle in the banner, or choose "Edit name" in the "…" menu. Both open the existing name-entry screen. Pull-to-refresh replaces the old refresh button. The runner row is removed.
  • Each button is at least 72dp tall, well above the 48dp touch minimum.
  • Existing casual "hold to go" behaviour is not covered here. It would need a decision on whether Go keeps hold-to-confirm.
